Laboratory description

The sample collection and coding laboratory is the starting point in the work system at the Central Drinking Water Laboratory, as it organizes the processes of drawing and receiving samples, preparing them, and coding them according to specific procedures that ensure the preservation of the sample’s nature, integrity, and traceability throughout the stages of examination and analysis. The laboratory operates according to a system that takes into account the nature and type of each sample and the requirements of the analysis required to be performed, ensuring that the samples reach the relevant technical departments in the appropriate condition to conduct the required analyses.

The role of the laboratory

The sample collection and coding laboratory represents the primary link between the sample collection sites and the various technical departments in the central laboratory, as it ensures the application of the correct procedures for collecting, transporting, receiving, recording, coding, and distributing samples to the relevant departments. It also contributes to preserving the integrity of samples, preventing confusion or loss of data, and providing accurate data and information about the source of the sample, date and time of withdrawal, and its type, which enhances the traceability and reliability of the final results and supports the drinking water quality monitoring system.

Laboratory tests

The laboratory performs field analyzes that include:

1.Measurement of residual chlorine

2.pH measurement

3.Measurement of electrical conductivity

4.Turbidity measurement

It is also concerned with the work related to collecting samples, receiving them, coding them, preserving them, transporting them, and distributing them to the relevant technical departments, and includes:

Preparing and implementing sampling plans and programs in accordance with monitoring and analysis requirements.

Drawing drinking water samples from designated locations and withdrawal points.

Ensure that the appropriate glassware is used for each type of analysis.

Record sample data, locations, dates and times of collection.

Coding the samples and giving them an identification number ensures easy tracking.

Receiving samples and verifying their compliance with receiving requirements.

Checking the apparent condition of samples and containers upon receipt.

Recording samples in approved electronic records and systems.

Save samples and transfer them to the technical departments according to appropriate requirements.

Distributing samples to the relevant departments according to the type of analysis required.

The most important devices in the laboratory

Work in the sample collection and coding laboratory depends on a set of tools and equipment that help maintain the quality, safety, and traceability of the samples, the most important of which are:

Temperature measuring devices to monitor the conditions of storing and transporting samples when needed.

Custom sample transport boxes and cases (Ice Box) to preserve samples during transport.

Computers and sample data recording and management systems to document withdrawal, coding, receipt and distribution data.

Refrigerators and chemicals used in preservation when samples need to be preserved in specific conditions.

Specialized laboratory glassware suitable for various types of analyses.

Equipment in the laboratory

Device name: Residual Chlorine device

A brief overview of the device

It is used to measure the concentration of residual chlorine in drinking water in the field at the point of sample collection.

Measuring helps ensure that there is an appropriate level of chlorine throughout the water distribution process.

Measurement is done at the withdrawal site to maintain a true representation of the water condition at the time of sample collection.

The measurement result is recorded within the sample data to support water quality assessment and track laboratory analysis results.

Equipment in the laboratory

Device name: pH Meter

A brief overview of the device

It is used to measure the pH or alkalinity of water in the field while collecting samples.

It is an important measurement that may be affected by transportation and storage conditions, so it is preferable to measure it at the withdrawal site.

The measurement helps give a quick indication of the elemental chemical properties of the water.

The device reading is recorded within the sample data to provide integrated information to the technical departments responsible for analysis.

Equipment in the laboratory

Device name: Conductivity Meter

A brief overview of the device

It is used to measure the electrical conductivity of water in the field as an indicator of its content of dissolved substances and ions.

It provides a quick reading that helps track changes in the chemical properties of water between different intake locations.

The measurement is made at the point of withdrawal to obtain a reading that represents the actual condition of the sample at the time of collection.

The result is documented within the sample data to be used in evaluating the results and monitoring the quality of water sources and stations.

Equipment in the laboratory

Device name: Turbidimeter

A brief overview of the device

 It is used to measure the degree of turbidity of water in the field and quickly evaluate its clarity.

 It helps detect visible changes in water quality that may be related to the presence of suspended particles or materials.

 Field measurement provides an important initial indication of the condition of the water at the point of sampling.

 The reading is recorded with sample data to support tracking and comparison with laboratory test results.
