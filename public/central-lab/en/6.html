Laboratory description

The laboratory is a specialized unit equipped in accordance with modern technical requirements to conduct a comprehensive set of bacteriological tests. It includes laboratory equipment and capabilities that support the implementation of bacteriological analyzes accurately and efficiently, in accordance with operating and quality control procedures appropriate to the nature of the tests. The laboratory includes qualified and trained scientific and technical cadres of specialists in the field of bacteriology.

The role of the laboratory

Receiving, recording, and preserving samples and conducting bacteriological tests and analyses. The tests are conducted according to the applicable analytical methods and procedures, and in accordance with the relevant international references and standards, and the guidelines of the World Health Organization (WHO), in addition to the standards, specifications, and requirements issued by the Egyptian Ministry of Health and Population and the competent national authorities. Our goal is to provide reliable analytical services and accurate results that contribute to supporting the drinking water quality control system, verifying its microbiological safety, and enhancing public health protection and community confidence in water quality.

Laboratory tests

The most important tests conducted by the laboratory to evaluate the microbiological quality of drinking water include:

Detection of total coliform bacteria

Detection of fecal coliform bacteria

Detection of fecal streptococci group

Conducting total bacterial count tests

Conducting various other bacteriological and microbiological tests for any external sample according to the nature of the sample and examination requirements.
