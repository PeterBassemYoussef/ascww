Laboratory description

One of the laboratories specialized in monitoring the quality and safety of drinking water, as it specializes in conducting microscopic examinations of water samples to detect microorganisms and biological pollutants that may affect the quality and safety of water, the most important of which are protozoa, algae, and protozoan worms and their various stages.

It is a specialized unit Conducting microscopic examinations of drinking water samples With the aim of detecting and identifying microorganisms, especially protozoa, algae, protozoa and their stages, using appropriate sample preparation methods and microscopic examination techniques and in accordance with approved standards and procedures.

The biological laboratory consists of: a microscopic examination laboratory for paracytology and a laboratory for algae examination and counting.

The role of the laboratory

The biological laboratory represents an important part of water quality monitoring programs, because it helps in the early detection of biological pollutants and evaluates the efficiency of water treatment and purification processes, which contributes to providing safe water suitable for human consumption.

Laboratory tests

1.General microscopic examination of drinking water samples.

2.Examination of protozoa and protozoa.

3.Examining algae and monitoring their types and density.

4.Examination of primary worms and their different stages.

5.Examination after filtration and concentration operations when needed to increase detection sensitivity.

6.Documenting microscopic findings with photographs when appropriate capabilities are available.

Manifold device

Manifold (Manifold device for vacuum filtration Vacuum Filtration ManifoldIt is used very importantly in examining water samples, especially when performing microscopic examination.

First: Using the Manifold device for microscopic examination of water

The device is used to filter and concentrate microorganisms present in water samples so that they can be collected on the surface of the filter paper and then examined microscopically.

Its most important uses are:

Filtering water samples and concentrating the microorganisms present in them, especially when they are present in small numbers.

Collecting algae present in the water on the surface of the filter paper, then examining it under a microscope to identify its types and estimate its density.

Detection of protozoa and parasites or their stages present in water samples, after concentration by filtration.

Detection of eggs, cysts and larvae of some parasites and worms present in water, according to the examination method and the type of filter paper used.

Concentrating bacteria and microorganisms present in a large volume of water, then using the filter for appropriate microscopic or microbiological examination.

Examination of drinking water to ensure that it is free of microorganisms or algae and parasites targeted in the screening program.

Examination of raw water and treated water samples in drinking water purification and treatment plants.

It can be used in environmental studies to compare the presence and diversity of microorganisms between different water samples.
