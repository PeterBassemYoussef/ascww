Laboratory description

The inorganic laboratory is one of the departments of the Central Laboratory for Drinking Water. The laboratory specializes in assessing all ions, salts, and heavy elements in drinking water, in addition to physical analyzes as well as the operational elements of drinking water plants. Device name:

(Ion Chromatography)

Ion chromatography

model : TITRIC FLEX 940-2500 Professional IC Vario

A brief overview of the device

A dual device that measures alkalinity, electrical conductivity, and pH, in addition to measuring inorganic elements (anions, cations, oxalides) through ionic separation to ensure their conformity with Egyptian standard specifications because of their impact on public health.

Ion chromatography (IC) is one of the important analytical techniques in the field of water quality monitoring, because of the ability it provides to separate and measure many anions and cations with appropriate accuracy and sensitivity.
The IC technique relies on separating the ions present in the sample according to their interaction with the ion separation column
Where a small amount of sample (20 micro) is injected into the device, then it passes through the separation column that separates the different ions, and it is detected and the concentration of each ion is determined in mg/L ((

The ions that are measured on ICDevice

Cations : Such as lithium (Li+), sodium (Na+), potassium (K+), calcium (+2Ca), magnesium (+2Mg), ammonium (NH4+).

Anions : Such as nitrate (-NO3), nitrite (NO2-), chloride (-Cl), sulfate (SO4+2), fluoride (F-),

Bromide (- Br), phosphate (PO+34)

Oxyhalide: Such as bromate (BrO-3), chlorite (CLO-2), and chlorate (CLO-3).

Device name: ICP-MS

)mass induction coupled plasma)

model : ICAP RQ

device (ICP MS It is one of the latest and most accurate measuring devices for detecting heavy metals

The device measures all heavy metals likely to be present in water, as it measures more than 20 heavy elements

In a very short time and with high accuracy, with precise concentrations of micrograms/liter

The device relies on producing plasma from argon gas at a very high temperature of up to 10,000 K. The plasma excites the ions and measures the characteristic mass of each element. Using the detector, it calculates the concentration of each element with extreme accuracy.

Device name:

Spectrophotometer

Spectrophotometer

model : Analyticaljina Specord 210 plus Double-Beam, HACH DR6000 Split Beam

A device used to measure the concentration of substances and elements in drinking water samples based on measuring the amount of light absorbed by the substance at a specific wavelength

It is characterized by very high sensitivity and accuracy to measure the concentration of some chemicals that have an impact on human health in drinking water and the extent of their compliance with the permissible limits according to the Egyptian Standard Specification for Drinking Water No. 182 of 2025.

 It measures iron (Fe), manganese (Mn), aluminum (AL), nitrate (NO3), nitrite (NO2), and ammonia (NH3).

There are two types of spectrophotometer devices, based on the design of the light beam used in the measurement, which are single-beam and dual-beam.
