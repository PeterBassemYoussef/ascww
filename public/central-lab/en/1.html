Central Drinking Water Plant

An integrated laboratory facility serving water quality and safety

The Central Drinking Water Plant is one of the most modern specialized laboratories in the country. It was established on an area of 1,200 square meters, with joint funding from the United States Agency for International Development within the Integrated Water Solutions Technical Support Project (IWSSTA), the National Authority for Drinking Water and Wastewater in Assiut, and the Water and Wastewater Company in Assiut, at a total cost of about 120 million pounds. Its implementation began in 2018 and was opened in December 2023.

The laboratory aims to conduct the necessary laboratory analyzes and tests to monitor the quality of drinking water in Assiut Governorate, in a way that supports the provision of high-quality and safe water to citizens, in accordance with the approved standards and specifications for potable water, and within the framework of Minister of Health and Population Resolution No. 182 of 2025 and the relevant standards and requirements, and in a way that ensures coverage of all standards and analyzes contained in the relevant legislation, as the laboratory covers the following:

Advanced capabilities to serve the governorates of Upper Egypt

The laboratory includes a group of modern analytical devices and techniques, which support its ability to conduct various types of analyzes accurately, efficiently and reliably. The laboratory serves Assiut Governorate, and has the capabilities that allow it to receive and analyze samples received from parties outside the company.

The laboratory also includes an elite group of human resources through an integrated work team that includes scientific competencies and distinguished laboratory experiences, ranging from chemists, microbiologists, technicians, and support services, in order to ensure the optimal operation of all components of the central drinking water plant.

Through the integration of human expertise, technical capabilities, and modern technologies, the central laboratory represents a specialized system to support monitoring the quality of drinking water, and provide reliable analytical results that contribute to protecting public health and enhancing citizens’ confidence in the quality of the water provided.
