Description of management

Quality management at the Central Drinking Water Laboratory is one of the basic pillars for ensuring the accuracy and reliability of laboratory analysis results, and supporting the drinking water safety and quality system, by applying the best international practices and standards in laboratory examination, analysis, and control.

Quality...the first line of defense for drinking water safety

Quality management contributes to supporting the drinking water safety system by ensuring that the results of chemical, physical, microbiological, and biological analyzes reflect the actual condition of the samples being examined, which helps quickly detect any changes or non-conforming indicators, and take appropriate measures in a timely manner.

International standards...and laboratory efficiency

The central laboratory applies a quality management system that complies with the requirements of the international standard ISO/IEC 17025 for the efficiency of testing and calibration laboratories, in addition to relying on specifications and standard methods approved in the field of drinking water analyses, which enhances confidence in the results and supports decision-making based on reliable scientific data.

Digitizing quality... towards a more efficient laboratory

Quality management supports the expansion of digital transformation and laboratory data management, which contributes to enhancing traceability, reducing errors, improving the speed of results circulation, raising the efficiency of operations, and supporting decision-making based on data and scientific evidence.

Management role

The laboratory's quality management system includes an integrated set of procedures and practices that ensure efficient performance and reliability of results, the most important of which are:

1- Make sure Hardware efficiency :(Calibration, maintenance, and verification of device performance).

2- Quality of reagents: Monitoring the validity of reagents and reference materials and their storage conditions.

3-Apply approved analysis methods and review their validity.

4-Internal quality control(Follow up on QC results and ensure the stability of the analytical process.

5-Proficiency testsParticipation in inter-comparison programs (proficiency tests).

6-Complete traceability of samples: Ensuring the integrity of samples and traceability from receipt until results are issued

7-Review before approval: Verifying, reviewing and approving the results according to the specified terms of reference.

8-Documented documents and records: Manage documents and records and ensure the availability of updated information.

9-Handling non-conformities and complaints and implementing corrective actions
