First: Introduction to the organic laboratory

The organic laboratory is one of the specialized departments of the Central Drinking Water Laboratory at the Water and Wastewater Company in Assiut and New Valley. It specializes in detecting and measuring pollutants and organic compounds in water samples, using advanced analytical techniques, with the aim of assessing water quality, monitoring potential pollutants, and supporting decision-making to ensure that drinking water conforms to approved specifications.

In total 174 compounds are estimated and measured from them 112 compound Organic By decision of the Minister of Health No. 182 of 2025

Laboratory role:

The organic laboratory specializes in conducting a wide range of advanced analyzes to detect and measure various organic compounds in **drinking water and raw water**, using gas and liquid chromatography techniques, and mass spectrometry, in addition to total analyzes of organic matter.

The laboratory's scope of work includes analyzing various groups of compounds, such as pesticides Insecticides and agricultural pesticides, Pollutants resulting from the leakage of oils and diesel into various waterways (Phenolic compounds), andaromatic compounds (Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons), volatile organic compounds, in addition to indicators of total organic content.

2 -AOX Analyzer

Adsorbable Organic Halogens Analyzer

Adsorptive organic halogens measuring device

Manufacturer: TE Instruments – Netherlands

Model: XPREP-3, Xplorer-TX

Description:

The device is used to measure total adsorbable organic halogens in water.

AOX represents an aggregate index for a group of organic compounds that contain halogens, such as chlorine, bromine, and others.

It is scientifically important to clarify that the device:

It does not identify each compound individually, but rather gives an overall measure of the content of adsorbable organohalogens.

Concentrations: nanograms per liter.

Its own analyses:

Adsorbable Organic Halogens – AOX.
